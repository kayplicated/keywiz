The Arcade
The arcade at the end of the pier still ran on quarters and stubbornness. Half the cabinets were dark, but the old space shooter in the corner glowed like a shrine, its high score table untouched since 1987: JAX with 983,450 points, then ZED, then KIM. Every summer a new kid tried to break it. Every summer they fed it their allowance, jaws clenched, hands sweating on the joystick, and every summer JAX stayed on top, a ghost in eight-bit letters.
