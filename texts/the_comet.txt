The Comet
Every seventy-six years the comet came back, and every seventy-six years almost nobody who remembered the last visit was still around to see it. Her grandmother had watched it as a girl from a hayfield, wrapped in a wool blanket. Now she watched it from the same field, wrapped in the same blanket, frayed at the edges. The tail stretched across the sky like a brushstroke of frost. She took a photo for someone she would never meet.
