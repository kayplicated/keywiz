The Tide Pool
At low tide the rocks became a museum nobody had to pay to enter. Anemones folded their green fingers shut as she leaned closer, and a tiny crab sidestepped behind a curtain of kelp, offended by her shadow. Periwinkles crawled at a pace that made a clock look reckless. She stayed until the water began to creep back in, one patient wave at a time, and the little world disappeared again beneath the ocean, exactly where it belonged.
