The Soldering Iron
The kit arrived in a box no bigger than a paperback: a bare circuit board, seventy diodes, a bag of switches, and a microcontroller the size of a stamp. He heated the iron, tinned the tip, and started on the first column. Every joint got a tiny cone of silver, shiny and smooth. By midnight his eyes ached and the room smelled of flux, but when he plugged it in and pressed a key, the letter appeared on the screen, and he grinned like a kid.
