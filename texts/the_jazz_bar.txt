The Jazz Bar
The jazz bar was hidden below a laundromat, down a staircase that smelled of soap and cigars. Inside, a quartet played under a single amber bulb: a sax, a double bass, brushes on a snare, and a piano that was slightly out of tune in exactly the right way. The bartender made a quick gin fizz without asking. Couples swayed between tables. A woman in a velvet jacket requested something blue and lazy, and the saxophonist closed his eyes and gave her exactly that.
