The Cabin
The snow started on Tuesday and simply never stopped. By Thursday the road had vanished, the power lines sagged, and the cabin was wrapped in a silence so thick it felt like wool. She fed the woodstove every two hours, made soup from whatever was left in the pantry, and read the same mystery novel twice. At night the wind moved through the pines like a slow tide. When the plow finally arrived on Sunday, she was almost disappointed to hear it.
