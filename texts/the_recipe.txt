The Recipe
Preheat the oven to 180 degrees. Whisk 3 eggs with 150 g of sugar until pale and fluffy, about 4 minutes. Fold in 200 g of flour, 1 tsp of baking powder, and a pinch of salt. Melt 120 g of butter and stir it in slowly, then add the zest of 2 lemons and 60 ml of juice. Pour into a 24 cm tin and bake for 35 to 40 minutes. Let it cool. Or don't; nobody has ever waited, and nobody ever will.
