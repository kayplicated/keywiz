The Pale Blue Dot
On its way out of the solar system, Voyager turned its camera around and took one last photograph back toward home. In that picture, the Earth is barely a single bright pixel, a pale blue mote suspended in a long beam of scattered sunlight. Every war ever fought, every love letter ever written, every sleeping child and barking dog, all of it is balanced on that one trembling speck. It is hard to look at the image for long without feeling, very gently, that we ought to be kinder to each other.
