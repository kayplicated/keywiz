The Neutron Star
When a heavy star runs out of fuel, its core can collapse so completely that the protons and electrons inside it merge, leaving a city-sized ball of almost pure neutrons. A single sugar cube of that material would weigh more than every human on Earth combined. Some of these tiny corpses spin hundreds of times a second, sweeping radio beams across the galaxy like impossibly precise lighthouses. Astronomers found the first one by accident, mistaking its perfect rhythm for a message from someone else.
