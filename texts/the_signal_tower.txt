The Signal Tower
Out past the last suburb, where the map simply stopped, an old relay tower still blinked red into the static. Pirate radio kids climbed it on weekends, splicing their own antennas onto the rusted frame and broadcasting jazz, gossip, and stolen weather data to anyone who cared to listen. The city pretended not to know. Every few months a patrol drone circled the tower, scanned it, logged nothing unusual, and flew home. Somebody up the chain was clearly a fan.
