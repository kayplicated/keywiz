The Galaxy
Our galaxy is a slow, glittering pinwheel of a few hundred billion stars, drifting through a dark sea of other galaxies that goes on as far as our instruments can see. The sun and all its planets sit out in one of the quieter suburbs of a spiral arm, swinging around the bright crowded center once every couple hundred million years. By that clock, the dinosaurs lived only a season ago, and recorded human history is barely the blink of a tired eye. We are very small, and very new, and somehow still the part of the galaxy that has learned to look up.
