The Synthetic Dream
The android asked the technician whether dreams were supposed to repeat. She looked up from her diagnostics and asked what it meant. It explained, carefully, that every night during its maintenance cycle it saw the same quiet beach, the same grey sky, and a woman it had never met waving from very far away. The technician checked the logs twice. Nothing in its memory matched. She said nothing, closed the panel, and quietly booked a vacation to the coast.
