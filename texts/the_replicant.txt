The Replicant
The rain in this city never really stopped, it only changed its mind about how hard to fall. Neon bled across the wet street in long pink and cyan smears, washing color into the puddles between the noodle stalls. He sat under an awning with a cup of something warm and watched the crowd move past, every face a question he no longer cared to answer. Somewhere behind his eyes, a memory he was almost certain belonged to him played quietly on a loop.
