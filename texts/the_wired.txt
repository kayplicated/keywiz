The Wired
She slipped the cable into the port behind her ear and the room went soft at the edges. The wired bloomed open around her, a quiet city of voices and half-remembered faces, layered over the dim light of her bedroom. Somewhere in that humming dark, a version of herself she had never written was already speaking to strangers in her name. The line between the girl at the desk and the girl on the network had gone thin enough to read through.
