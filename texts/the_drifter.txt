The Drifter
The transport had seen better decades, but she still answered the throttle when it mattered. Her captain knew every rattle and groan, the way a rancher knows the moods of an old horse. Out past the inner planets, the lawmen got thinner and the contracts got stranger, and a quiet hold and a friendly smile were worth more than a loud gun. They lit out for the rim with half a manifest and a full tank, chasing whatever paid in coin instead of promises.
