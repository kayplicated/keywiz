The Terraformer
On the third century of the project, the first rain fell on Kestrel Basin. It came down thin and strange, tasting faintly of iron, and the engineers stood outside without their helmets for the first time, faces turned up, laughing like children. The lichen fields had done their slow, invisible work. Far off, the atmospheric processors kept humming, as they had for longer than anyone alive. Someone had planted a single maple sapling by the dome. It was already leaning toward the light.
