The Mech Bay
The mech bay was a cathedral of scaffolds, sparks, and swearing. Twelve-meter walkers stood in their cradles with their chest plates open, cables spilling out like tangled vines, while crews in orange jumpsuits crawled over them with welding torches and coffee. Unit KX-9 had come back from the frontier with half a leg and a cockpit full of sand. The pilot sat on an upturned crate nearby, staring at nothing, while the mechanics quietly argued about whether to fix it or let it rest.
