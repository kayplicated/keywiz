The Night Shift
At three in the morning the data center was the loudest quiet place on earth. Rows of servers breathed warm air into the cold aisles, their status lights blinking green in endless, patient rhythm. The technician on night shift walked her rounds with a flashlight and a clipboard, checking temperatures and listening for the one fan that sounded wrong. Somewhere in those racks, millions of people were sending messages, buying shoes, and falling asleep to videos, and none of them knew she was there.
