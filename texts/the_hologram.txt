The Hologram
The noodle stand had a hologram for a mascot, a cartoon fox that waved at passing drones and had glitched into speaking only in old song lyrics. Nobody had the heart to fix it. Office workers in mirrored visors lined up beneath it at midnight, steam rising from their bowls into the flickering blue of its tail. When the rain came, the fox sang about rain. When the power dipped, it froze mid-wink, and the whole street waited, quietly, for it to come back.
