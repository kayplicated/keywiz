The Rooftop
Rain hissed on the neon signs below, turning the whole district into a smear of pink and electric blue. She sat on the edge of the rooftop with her boots dangling over sixty floors of nothing, waiting for the courier drone. It arrived ten minutes late, buzzing like an angry wasp, and dropped a small black case into her lap. No note, no sender, just a blinking amber light and a lock that wanted her thumbprint. She hesitated, then pressed down.
