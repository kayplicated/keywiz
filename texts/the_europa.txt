The Europa
Europa is one of the smaller moons of Jupiter, wrapped in a smooth shell of cracked, frozen water that gleams like an old porcelain plate. Beneath that crust, tides from its giant parent keep flexing the moon, and that constant kneading appears to hold a salty ocean liquid in the dark. There may be more water under that ice than in every sea on Earth put together. Whether anything in that buried ocean has ever stirred, learned, or dreamed is one of the more patient questions our species is currently asking.
