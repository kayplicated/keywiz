The Voyager
The two Voyager probes left Earth in the late seventies with a tour of the outer planets in mind, and they have been falling outward ever since. Each one carries a golden record, etched with greetings in dozens of languages, the song of a humpback whale, and the brainwaves of a young woman in love. Both have crossed the bubble of solar wind that surrounds our sun, becoming the first human objects to drift in true interstellar space. They will outlive every city we have ever built, quietly carrying our voices into a dark that does not know us yet.
