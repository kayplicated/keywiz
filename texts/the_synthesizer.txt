The Synthesizer
The old synthesizer took up half the desk and weighed more than the desk itself. It had forty-four knobs, a cracked wooden panel, and no memory at all; every sound had to be built from scratch, every time. She twisted the filter cutoff and a buzzing sawtooth wave melted into something warm and hollow. A little resonance, a slow envelope, a hint of vibrato. When she pressed a low chord, the speakers hummed, the windows rattled, and the cat left the room in disgust.
