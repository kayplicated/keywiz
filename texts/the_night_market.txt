The Night Market
The alley smelled like fryer oil and ozone, and every stall sold something the law had a strong opinion about. A vendor in a cracked visor was hawking refurbished memory, wiped almost clean, almost. Someone two stalls down had a basket of stim patches and a parrot that swore in three languages. She kept her hood up, her credits buried deep, and her smile small, and she let the noise of the market carry her like a slow river toward the back room she had come to find.
