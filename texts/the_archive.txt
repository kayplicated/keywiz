The Archive
The archivist of Station Kepler kept the last paper books in a sealed vault beneath the hydroponics deck. Once a week she put on cotton gloves and turned their brittle pages, one by one, so the spines would not forget how to bend. Most of the crew thought it was a quaint waste of oxygen. But when the main servers failed during the long blackout, it was her vault they came to, quietly, asking how to purify water and set a broken arm.
