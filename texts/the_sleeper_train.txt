The Sleeper Train
The night train rocked gently through the mountains, its wheels counting the miles in a steady, sleepy rhythm. In the narrow cabin, she lay on the top bunk and watched villages slide past the window, each one a scatter of yellow lights that appeared and vanished like a thought. Somewhere down the corridor a child was laughing, and a conductor knocked softly on a door. By the time she woke, the snow had turned to vineyards and the air smelled of warm bread.
