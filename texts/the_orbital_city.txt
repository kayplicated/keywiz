The Orbital City
From the observation ring you could watch the whole city turn beneath you: parks curling up the inside of the cylinder, rivers that climbed the horizon, rooftops hanging overhead like a ceiling of streets. New arrivals always grabbed the railing the first time. Locals just sipped their coffee. At dusk the long mirrors tilted away from the sun, and the lights came on in a slow wave from one end to the other, like a city breathing out after a very long day.
