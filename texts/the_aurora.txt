The Aurora
The aurora is what happens when the sun throws a tantrum and our planet shrugs it off in color. Charged particles blown out from the sun get caught in the magnetic field around the Earth, funneled toward the poles, and slammed into the thin air high above. The atoms up there glow as they recover, oxygen in soft greens and blood reds, nitrogen in cool blues and trembling violets. From the ground it looks like a curtain being shaken by a quiet hand, but it is really our planet shielding us, and showing its work.
