The Ferry
The first ferry left before sunrise, its engine coughing awake while gulls argued over the railing. A handful of commuters stood on the deck with paper cups of coffee, saying nothing, watching the harbor lights shrink behind them. The water was flat and grey as pewter. Halfway across, the sun broke over the far hills and every window on the island caught fire at once. Nobody cheered, but everybody looked up, and for a moment the whole boat seemed to hold its breath.
