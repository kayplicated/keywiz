The Dust Storm
The forecast called it a regional event, which on Mars meant the sky would turn the color of rust for six weeks. Inside the habitat, the crew taped up the seals, dimmed the lights, and rationed power from the battery bank. Outside, the solar panels vanished under a fine red powder that crept into every joint and hinge. They played cards, fixed what broke, and recorded long messages home, knowing they would arrive twenty minutes late and sound braver than anyone felt.
