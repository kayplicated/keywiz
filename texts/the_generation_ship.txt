The Generation Ship
Nobody on board had ever seen the sky their grandparents left behind, and nobody alive would see the one they were aimed at. The ship hummed through the dark with its forests and its rivers folded carefully inside its ribs, a small green world pretending to be still. Children grew up calling the curved ceiling the sky and the maintenance lamps the sun, and the words fit better with each generation. Out past the hull, the stars crawled by so slowly that they might as well have been painted there.
