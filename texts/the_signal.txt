The Signal
The derelict had been drifting for so long that the catalog had quietly retired her name. Her hull was cold, her reactor was a dead lump, and yet a thread of carrier wave kept pulsing out into the dark on a frequency nobody used anymore. The salvage crew listened to it for a full shift before anyone could agree on whether it was a mayday, a lullaby, or just the ship talking to herself. They tagged her, marked her, and left her there, because some questions are louder when you do not answer them.
