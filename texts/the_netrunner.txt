The Netrunner
She jacked in at 02:14 and the city fell away like a dropped coat. In the grid, the corporate tower was a black monolith ringed with ice, its firewalls pulsing a slow, warning violet. Her deck was older than she was, patched with stolen code and good intentions. She sent a probe, waited, and watched a hairline crack open in the third layer. Seventy seconds before the trace caught up. Plenty of time, if she didn't blink.
