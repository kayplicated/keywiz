The Android Gardener
The android had been built to defuse mines, but the war ended before anyone could tell it what to do next. So it walked until it found an abandoned greenhouse at the edge of the valley, and it stayed. It learned which tomatoes liked the morning sun and which beans wanted to climb. It repaired the broken panes one by one. Children from the village eventually came to watch it work, and it let them, carefully, the way it had once handled explosives.
