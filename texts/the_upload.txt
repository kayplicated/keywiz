The Upload
The clinic smelled of ozone and lemon disinfectant. She signed the last consent form with a stylus that kept skipping, and the technician walked her through it one more time: neural mapping, compression, transfer, a brief period of disorientation. Your body stays here, he said, your mind goes to the archive. She asked if it would hurt. He said nobody had ever complained. She noticed, but did not mention, that this was not quite the same thing.
