The Cryosleep
Waking from cryosleep felt like surfacing through a lake of cold syrup. The pod hissed open, frost curling off the glass, and the ship's voice said good morning in a tone that suggested it had been practicing for decades. Her joints ached. Her mouth tasted of copper. On the console a single line blinked patiently: elapsed time, 41 years, 7 months, 12 days. Outside the viewport, a pale green planet hung in the dark, closer than any dream.
